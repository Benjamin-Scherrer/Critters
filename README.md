# Critters
## Abstract
Move animated geometrical bodies with playful interactions and combine them to imaginative creatures. 
Individuals coalesce into dynamic unity. 
This playful interaction is based on the modular design of Sophie Taeuber-Arp's puppets, 
which inspire through their clear shape language and flexible combinability.

Gierer Damian, Häfliger Nadja, Scherrer Benjamin, Staub Michael

## Project
Critters, previously called Locketry, is a digital exhibition piece made at ZHdK as part of the Minor Digital Play.
Critters was developped in cooperation with the Museum of Design Zurich for the exhibition 'Museum of the Future'.
